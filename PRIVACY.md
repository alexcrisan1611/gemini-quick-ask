# Privacy Policy — Quick Ask for Gemini

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Quick Ask for Gemini is a browser extension that sends text you choose to Google Gemini. This policy explains what it handles and where that data goes.

## What the extension handles

- **Text and addresses you choose to send.** When you use a right-click item or a keyboard shortcut, the extension reads the selected text, the link, or the address of the current page. It reads this only at the moment you ask, and only from the tab you're on.
- **Your settings.** Your preferences and custom actions.

## Where it goes

- **To Gemini, only when you ask.** The extension opens `gemini.google.com` and types your prompt there. From that point, Google's own terms and privacy policy apply.
- **Settings stay in Chrome.** Settings are saved with Chrome's storage and sync between your own Chrome browsers if you use Chrome sync. A prompt waiting to be typed is kept in local Chrome storage for a few seconds and deleted once it's used.

## What it doesn't do

- It has no servers and sends nothing to the developer.
- It doesn't collect analytics, track browsing, or read pages you don't ask about.
- It doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

## Permissions

- **Context menus:** adds the Ask items to the right-click menu.
- **Storage:** saves your settings and passes your prompt to the Gemini tab.
- **Active tab and scripting:** when you press a shortcut, reads the selected text and address of the current tab.
- **gemini.google.com:** types and sends your prompt in Gemini.
