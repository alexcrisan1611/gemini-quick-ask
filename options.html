General

How Gemini opens when you ask something.

Right-click menu

Choose which items appear when you right-click.

"Ask Gemini about this page" appears directly in Chrome's menu, because it's the only item there. On selected text and links, Chrome groups the items under a Quick Ask for Gemini submenu, since there's more than one.

Keyboard shortcuts

Ask Gemini without touching the mouse. Shortcuts follow your General settings.

Ask and open

Ask Gemini about this page Sends the address of the page you're on.
Ask Gemini about selected text Uses the page instead if nothing is selected.
Open Gemini Opens Gemini, or switches to it.
Open the popup The same panel you get by clicking the toolbar icon.

Action shortcuts

Choose which action each shortcut runs. It uses the selected text, or the page if nothing is selected.

Actions

Quick Ask for Gemini

Send anything on the web to Gemini in one step. It opens Gemini, types your prompt, and sends it for you.

What it can do

Ask about anything

Right-click selected text, a link, or empty space on a page. Gemini opens with your question already sent.

Actions

Summarize, explain, translate, fact-check, or find counterarguments in one click. Edit them or add your own.

Keyboard shortcuts

Skip the menu entirely.

  • Ask about this page
  • Ask about selection
  • Open Gemini

Works your way

Reuse one Gemini tab, review prompts before they're sent, stay on your page while Gemini works, and pick which Google account to use. Change settings.

How it works

  1. You right-click something, or press a shortcut.
  2. Gemini opens on your chosen account, in a new tab or the one you reuse.
  3. Your prompt is typed in and sent. You'll see a small status note in the corner while it happens.

Privacy

Quick Ask for Gemini has no servers and collects nothing. Your settings sync through your Chrome profile. Text only leaves your browser when you send it to Gemini, and Google's Gemini terms apply from there. Read the privacy policy.
